# Style bible — worked example

Reference: a 17.9 s vertical explainer reel (720 x 1280, 29.97 fps, 7 shots) about decision-making, with kinetic type, a counting number, a puzzle-piece morph, a growing bar chart and a target struck by a dart. Measured with ffprobe and two contact sheets at 2 fps; colours sampled from frames.

```
STYLE BIBLE
1. Palette: light grey paper #DADADA (volume, most shots) and white #FFFFFF (opening shots); deep indigo #210B9B (primary: boxes, bold words); violet #7A16C1 → #A460A8 gradient (shapes, 4-point stars); warm orange #EAA242 with a soft glow (numbers, highlights); lime #B2D466 (one small glossy sphere, a rare accent); near-black #1F1F24 for serif text.
2. Render look: clean 2.5D motion graphics. Flat matte shapes with subtle gradients and short soft drop shadows, plus one black-and-white photoreal cut-out per shot (a brain, a dartboard).
3. Background: flat paper with a fine 1 px grey grid (about 1/30 of frame width per square), soft vignette; broad pale grey arc strokes sweep across; oversized 4-point stars cropped by the frame corners.
4. Typography: two faces always paired. A heavy geometric sans (indigo or charcoal, tight tracking) for key words, a light italic serif for connecting words; one condensed heavy sans for a headline moment. Sentence case. Words stack in 2–3 lines.
5. Text reveal: word by word out of a horizontal or vertical blur, about 4 words per second; emphasis words larger and bolder.
6. Motion language: entries ease out with a small overshoot; shapes morph (circle → rounded diamond → puzzle piece); numbers count up for about 2 s then hold; bars grow from a baseline with 0.1 s stagger.
7. Transitions: whip-left with horizontal blur, scroll-up, scale-push past the frame, and a few hard cuts.
8. Pace: 7 shots in 17.9 s, about 2.6 s per shot; one idea per shot.
9. Camera: square-on to a flat plane, mostly static; short drifts and pushes only.
10. Lighting and finish: one soft key from the upper left, visible only as short shadows; soft glow on orange numbers; no film grain; crisp edges.
```

Style only. Left out on purpose: the reel's own words ("Did You Know?", "Problem Solving"), any creator handle or watermark, and its subject matter.

Layout note for a 16:9 output from this 9:16 reference: stacked words move to the upper-left third, the main object sits in the right two-thirds, decoration crops at the top-right and bottom-left corners.
